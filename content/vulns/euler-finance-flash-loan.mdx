## Summary

On March 13, 2023, Euler Finance suffered a flash loan attack resulting in the loss of approximately $197 million across multiple assets. The attacker exploited a vulnerability in the Euler Protocol's donate functionality to manipulate the internal accounting of the protocol.

## Root Cause

The vulnerability existed in the `donate()` function of the Euler protocol. When users donated assets directly to the protocol, the protocol failed to properly update the internal accounting of existing positions. This allowed the attacker to:

1. Take flash loans to deposit large amounts of collateral
2. Donate the borrowed funds to inflate the value of existing positions
3. Liquidate the inflated positions for profit

The core issue was that donations were not accounted for in the risk calculations, creating an imbalance between the protocol's internal bookkeeping and the actual token balances.

## Impact

- Total loss: ~$197M across ETH, WBTC, USDC, DAI, and stETH
- Attacker drained funds from multiple Euler pools
- Governance tokens and other protocol assets were also affected

## Remediation

Euler Finance deployed patches to:
- Remove or restrict the donate function
- Add proper accounting checks for any value transferred to the protocol
- Implement additional safeguards for flash loan interactions

The team also negotiated with the attacker and recovered a significant portion of the stolen funds through on-chain communication.
